Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 28, 2026

FreeCellMac is a native macOS card game. We designed it to work offline and to collect as little information as possible.

Information we collect

FreeCellMac does not collect, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Information stored on your Mac

Game preferences, the current session, undo history, and saved game files are stored locally on your Mac when you use those features. These files are not uploaded by FreeCellMac or synchronized to a server.

Purchases

If you purchase FreeCell Pro, Apple processes the transaction through StoreKit and the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple may process transaction and account information under its own privacy policy.

Children’s privacy

FreeCellMac does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above will be updated with any revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the support page and open an issue in the project’s public support tracker. Please do not include passwords, payment information, or other sensitive data in a support request.
