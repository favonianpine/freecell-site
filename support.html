When reporting a problem

Please include the app version, macOS version, the game number if relevant, and the steps that reproduce the issue. Screenshots are helpful for visual problems.

Do not include passwords, payment details, private save files, or other sensitive information in an issue.

Common questions

Does FreeCellMac need an account or an internet connection?

No. The game and its local save features work offline. An internet connection is only needed for App Store transactions and restoring purchases through Apple.

What does FreeCell Pro include?

FreeCell Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks the complete solver and unlimited undo.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the in-app Pro purchase panel and choose Restore Purchases. StoreKit verifies the purchase with Apple; no receipt or payment information is sent to us.
